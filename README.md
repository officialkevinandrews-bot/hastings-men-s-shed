# Hastings Men’s Shed

Create a brand new website for Hastings & St Leonards Men's Shed (UK Registered Charity CIO, https://www.hastingsmenshed.org.uk/) to replace their outdated site with a professional, warm, and highly engaging digital home.

Key goals and requirements:
1. Primary focus: Welcoming new members and making visiting easy and reassuring, while also prominently highlighting the charity's local impact in combating social isolation and loneliness through practical craft and community.
2. Branding & Colors: Root the design palette in their official logo colors (deep teal, cyan/aqua, warm yellow/gold, charcoal text) paired with warm off-whites and natural workshop textures. The current website colors do not match the brand well; make this new site feel modern, clean, accessible, and grounded in the logo's identity.
3. Content & Ethos: Take the writing and information from https://www.hastingsmenshed.org.uk/ and amplify it with warmth, clarity, and readability:
   - Who we are & our mission: wood and metal workshops, hobby projects, sharing skills safely, mitigating loneliness, open to everyone 18+ (men and women welcome).
   - Visiting & Joining: Drop-in anytime during open hours, grab a cuppa, low-pressure atmosphere. Very affordable fees: £10 one-off joining fee, £3 minimum session contribution (includes tea and coffee).
   - Workshop times & location: The Godfrey Bevan Workshops, 20 Manhattan Gardens, Hastings, East Sussex TN35 5DQ. Phone: 01424 444246. Opening times: Monday 10am-1pm (Woodcarving), Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday 10am-4pm (General), 2nd & 4th Saturday 10am-1pm, Thursday & Sunday closed.
   - Community Impact, Testimonials & Supporters: Letters of support from High Sheriff Lucinda Fraser and Sally-Ann Hart, visits from Lord Lieutenant Andrew Blackman, partnerships with Big Local NE Hastings, The National Lottery Community Fund, Julia & Hans Rausing Trust, Sussex Community Foundation, Hastings Voluntary Action, Ore Community Centre & Sunflower Cafe, Ore in Bloom, Southern Water, and The Muffin Club.
   - Donations: SumUp donation integration/link (https://pay.sumup.com/b2c/QMPIUPBZ).
   - Policies & Constitution: Clear link/page for CIO constitution, charitable objects, and policies.
4. Assets & Images:
   - Logo: Use the attached manshedlogo.jpg.
   - 32 Project Photos (from the attached zip wetransfer_3-tier-fruit-bowl-jpg_2026-11-11_1228.zip): Unpack and showcase these across the site in an interactive "Made in the Shed" showcase categorized into Wildlife & Gardens (owl boxes, bird tables, hedgehog houses, insect boxes), Craft & Gifts (Art Deco clock, turned bowls, horse head, vases), and Community Commissions (Sensory Trail, HVA pigeon holes, bench refurbishments). Use the "Welcome to the Shed" photo as a featured welcome element.
   - Video (Hastings_and_St_Leonards_Mens_Shed_Slideshow.mp4): Include a dedicated "Take a Look Inside the Shed" interactive video player section where visitors can watch the reception slideshow.
5. Commission & Repair Enquiries:
   - Provide an enquiry form for custom woodwork, furniture repairs, and commissions.
   - Notifications: Configure notifications to send to officialkevinandrews@gmail.com for testing. Do NOT send to sales@hastingsmenshed.org.uk until explicitly instructed to switch over. Also store submitted enquiries in the database.
